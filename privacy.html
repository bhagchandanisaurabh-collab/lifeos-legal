Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains the handling of personal information by LifeOS, a privately operated, single-user application, especially its optional read-only bank-import feature.

1. Who operates LifeOS

LifeOS is used by its individual owner for personal record-keeping. It is not a public account-information service for other users. Privacy questions can be directed to saurabhbhagchandani49@gmail.com.

2. Information processed

If the owner chooses to link a personal bank account, LifeOS may receive the approved account identifiers (limited locally to account reference information such as the last four IBAN characters), account balances, transaction identifiers, transaction dates, amounts, currencies, descriptions, merchants or counterparties, and transaction status, to the extent the bank provides them. LifeOS also stores Finance entries and manual classifications created by the owner.

For this feature, LifeOS does not require or store the owner's Revolut password, card PIN, or payment authorization credentials. A bank session identifier is retained in encrypted form so that authorized read-only synchronization can occur.

3. Purpose and authorization

Bank information is used solely to help the owner view and reconcile personal balances, income, spending, transfers and transaction history within LifeOS. Account access is initiated only after the owner explicitly authorizes it through the relevant banking consent flow. Import decisions and classification may require the owner's review.

4. Where information is stored

In the current local installation, LifeOS stores its working database on the owner's computer, with local backups. Bank session details are encrypted using a key held separately from the database. The owner controls the computer, application, backups, and any future hosting configuration. If LifeOS is later deployed to an external server, this notice should be updated to describe that deployment before use.

5. External services

Enable Banking and Revolut: The optional bank connection uses Enable Banking to request account information from Revolut after authorization. Those organizations process information under their own privacy notices and terms. LifeOS requests account-information access, not payment initiation.

Google: If the owner configures Gmail for account recovery, the app uses a send-only Gmail permission to send verification or recovery messages. Google processes those emails in accordance with its services and policies. That is separate from the bank connector.

GitHub Pages: This legal-information website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It contains no LifeOS account database or bank records. The pages themselves do not load analytics scripts, advertising, or third-party trackers; GitHub may process ordinary website access information under its own policies.

6. Sharing

The owner does not sell financial information or provide it to advertisers. Information is exchanged with Enable Banking and the approved bank only as necessary for the authorized read-only connection. Information used for Gmail delivery is sent to Google when that optional feature is used.

7. Retention and deletion

Imported transactions remain in the LifeOS Finance records until the owner removes them. Disconnecting a bank stops future synchronization and removes the active connection, but it does not automatically erase historical transactions already imported. Copies may remain in backups until those backups are deleted according to the owner's retention choices.

8. Controls and security

The owner can choose which authorized accounts to import, review imported entries, disconnect from Enable Banking, and revoke bank access in Revolut. LifeOS uses authentication, encrypted bank session storage, and conservative transaction-import controls. No application can promise absolute security.

9. Changes

This policy describes the current personal-use setup as of the update date. Changes to the integration or its hosting may require this policy to be revised.

LifeOS is a private single-user application. These pages describe its personal bank-information integration. No app credentials or account data are published here.
