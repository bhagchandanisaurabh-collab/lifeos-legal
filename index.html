LifeOS legal information

Last updated: 21 November 2026

LifeOS is a private, single-user life-management application. Its optional Finance bank-import feature can read information from accounts the owner explicitly authorizes through Enable Banking, including personal Revolut accounts.

These public documents describe the bank-import feature and its terms. LifeOS is not offered here as a service to the public. The source code, private keys, OAuth tokens, financial data, and LifeOS database are not published on this site.

Read the Privacy PolicyRead the Terms of Service

What the integration does

  • Reads approved account information, balances and transactions for the owner's personal financial tracking.
  • Does not initiate payments or transfer money.
  • Allows the owner to disconnect and revoke authorization with the bank or Enable Banking.
